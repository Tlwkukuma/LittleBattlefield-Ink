## 完整生成提示词

Create ONE very SIMPLE, CHARMING CARTOON SOLDIER design for a small-scale 2.5D video game. The user has repeatedly rejected realistic, detailed, armored designs and now explicitly asks for MORE CARTOON, SIMPLE LINE DRAWING, FAR LESS COMPLEXITY. Start a fresh simplified design. This is a clean minimalist character concept, not a realistic soldier, not detailed comic-book armor.

STYLE: extremely economical hand-drawn cartoon, bold clean dark outlines, broad flat color fills, almost no internal lines. Like a charming character drawn with a few confident marker strokes. Readable instantly as a tiny game character. Matte flat illustration, simple rounded silhouette with a few mild angular corners. Only ONE small flat shadow shape per major body part, no gradients, no 3D rendering, no texture, no hatching, no surface noise, no scratches, no battle damage. Do NOT add decoration to fill empty surfaces.

PROPORTIONS: roughly THREE AND A HALF heads tall, noticeably large head, small stout torso, short sturdy legs, mitten-like hands and simple oversized boots. Friendly adult cartoon soldier, relaxed neutral expression, not muscular. Face is a single peach shape with two tiny dark oval eyes, one short nose stroke, one short mouth stroke. No eyebrows unless one simple short line, no stubble, no wrinkles, no lips, no ears with internal detail. Cute but not infant.

DESIGN CONSISTS OF ONLY THESE FIVE SIMPLE READABLE ELEMENTS:
1) A plain muted blue-gray helmet: one simple dome with a small flat brim. No goggles, no plates, no seams, no screws, no stripes, no badges.
2) A plain tan face.
3) A simple muted blue-gray uniform with ONE large warm ivory sleeveless vest panel. Vest is a plain broad trapezoid, no panel divisions, no buckles, no straps, no collar layers, no pockets. A single dark belt line may separate torso and trousers.
4) Plain khaki short sturdy trousers, maximum ONE small fold line at each knee.
5) Dark charcoal simple mitten gloves and plain chunky boots, no finger joints, no laces, no toe caps, no knee armor, no shin armor.

NO shoulder armor. NO forearm armor. NO pouches. NO backpack. NO gun. NO bandolier. NO tools. NO rivets. NO tactical details. NO labels. NO symbols. No floating props or decorative shapes.
Keep only five solid colors: desaturated blue-gray, warm ivory, tan skin, khaki, dark charcoal outlines/boots/gloves. Every large surface mostly empty and clean. The final character should feel at least 80% simpler than a typical detailed military comic character.

Single full-body character, near-front three-quarter view, neutral slight A-pose with hands away from torso, feet apart, both arms and both boots fully visible. One character only, centered with generous margins on pure white background. No ground shadow, no floor, no scenery, no UI, no text, no frame, no additional views. Portrait composition, clear silhouette. Prioritize SIMPLICITY and CARTOON CHARM over equipment, realism or technical detail.
