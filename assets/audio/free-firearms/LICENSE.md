# The Free Firearm Sound Library — selected game-ready clips

This notice covers the original field-shot source material. Additional CC0
sources and designed derivative clips in this directory are documented in
`ADDITIONAL_LICENSES.md` and `data/free-weapon-audio.json`.

Original authors: Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney.

- Selected source-file mirror: https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit
- Mirror revision: `a19b7458a593598211c95ec46ef4eb4b6d1f94d7`

The release page identifies the recordings as CC0 and permits personal and
professional use without royalties or required attribution. These credits are
retained voluntarily. No commercial game's extracted audio is included here.

These files are modified excerpts of the original field recordings: individual
discharges isolated with their natural tail, one recorded microphone channel
selected, low rumble removed, resampled to 48 kHz PCM16, peak normalized with
headroom and faded to silence. Pitch and shot timing are not shifted.

The mapping is not a claim that every represented game weapon was recorded.
Exact gun family / related cartridge / role substitutes are described in
`docs/free-weapon-audio.md`. The manifest `data/free-weapon-audio.json` records
each excerpt's original filename, source URL and SHA-256, microphone channel,
sample offsets, recording description, and derived-file SHA-256.
